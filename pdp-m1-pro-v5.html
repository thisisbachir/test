One place to train.

Smooth cable crossover system

Train with 15 adjustable positions and a smooth 2:1 pulley ratio for precise, controlled movement. The dual stainless-steel rail design and high-strength steel cables ensure durability for low/high crossover, press, rows, pushdowns, and more.

Professional Smith attachment system

The M1 PRO Smith system uses linear bearings and spring-back protection for smooth, secure lifting. With a 352 lb capacity and 11 height settings, it delivers reliable support for heavy bench, squats, and full-body strength training.

Maximize pull-up performance

The ergonomic 1.1" pull-up bar with a 2.36" upward bend enhances comfort and range of motion. With 82.8" of ground clearance, you get plenty of headroom for clean reps, while multiple grip options help you target different muscles—great for beginners and advanced athletes.

Enhanced flexibility and safety

Featuring 12 large adjustment holes, the M1 PRO lets you easily position J-hooks, safety bars, and dip bars. This setup improves versatility and stability, helping you train confidently across a wide range of strength exercises.

Effortless weight adjustment for enhanced workouts

Easily change resistance with the quick-select weight stack—no extra plates or storage needed. Just slide the pin and keep your focus on training. Great for beginners and anyone building strength.

Enhance your training with the weight carriage system

For advanced lifters, the weight carriage holds up to 308 lbs per side (440 lbs total). The 5° incline keeps plates secure, offering a safer, more stable setup for heavy training.

Your questions, answered.

How should I prepare for assembly?

Review the assembly manual before delivery, clear the full workout area, and plan for a second person to help position the larger frame parts.

Where can I find the assembly guides?

The M1 PRO and WSM1 manuals are linked in the product summary and shipping section. Keep both guides available when your boxes arrive.

Will the package arrive in one delivery?

No. The configuration ships in multiple labeled boxes, and boxes may arrive separately. Use the package-specific packing list above to check each delivery.

Can I add compatible attachments later?

Yes. Confirm the attachment model and M1 PRO compatibility on the product page before ordering; compatibility can vary by accessory.

What should I do if a box or part is missing?

Record the box label and missing part number, then contact RitFit support so the team can trace the shipment or arrange the correct replacement.

Where can I confirm warranty and return terms?

Use the current Warranty and Returns links in the footer, or contact RitFit support for terms that apply to your order and retailer.
